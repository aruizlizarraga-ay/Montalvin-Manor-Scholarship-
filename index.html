About the Award

Our Mission & Purpose

The Montalvin Manor Community Scholarship was established to honor the spirit of the neighborhood that has nurtured generations of Bay Area families. Born from the post-war American Dream and built on a foundation of resilience, diversity, and community pride, Montalvin Manor has always believed in investing in its people.

This scholarship recognizes graduating high school seniors and current college students who embody the values of Montalvin Manor — hard work, civic engagement, and commitment to uplifting the communities around them.

One (1) scholarship of $1,000 will be awarded annually to a deserving student with demonstrated ties to the Montalvin Manor community and a commitment to higher education.

Eligibility

Who Should Apply

Applicants must meet all of the following criteria to be considered for the Montalvin Manor Community Scholarship:

  • Current resident of Montalvin Manor, OR a graduate of a West Contra Costa Unified School District high school (including Pinole Valley, Kennedy, DeAnza, or Richmond High School)
  • Currently enrolled in, or accepted to, an accredited two-year college, four-year university, vocational program, or trade school
  • Minimum cumulative GPA of 2.5 (on a 4.0 scale) or equivalent
  • U.S. citizen or eligible non-citizen with lawful permanent residency
  • Demonstrated financial need (as evidenced by FAFSA or other documentation)
  • Has not previously received this scholarship (unless applying for renewal)

There is no restriction on field of study. All academic disciplines and vocational paths are equally valued and encouraged to apply.

Application Materials

What to Submit

A complete application package must include all of the following:

  • Completed Application Form — Download and complete the official Montalvin Manor Scholarship Application. All fields are required.
  • Official Transcript — An official or unofficial transcript from your most recent educational institution, showing cumulative GPA. Transcripts must be current within the last academic term.
  • Personal Essay — A personal statement of 400–600 words responding to one (1) of the essay prompts listed below. Essays must be submitted as a PDF or Word document.
  • Two (2) Letters of Recommendation — At least one letter must be from a teacher, counselor, or academic mentor. The second may be from a community member, employer, or coach who is not a family member.
  • Proof of Residency or Community Ties — A utility bill, school enrollment record, lease agreement, or other documentation confirming current or previous Montalvin Manor/West Contra Costa residency.
  • Financial Need Documentation — A copy of your Student Aid Report (SAR) from FAFSA submission, or a brief financial statement (1 page maximum) explaining your financial circumstances.

Incomplete applications will not be reviewed. All materials must be received by the deadline. Digital submissions are preferred; physical submissions must be postmarked by April 15. I love you Selena

Personal Essay

Essay Prompts (Choose One)

Respond to one of the following prompts in 400–600 words. Be specific, honest, and personal. The committee values authenticity over perfection.

Prompt 1 — Community & Roots

Montalvin Manor was built on the belief that every family deserves a place to call home. Describe how your community — whether Montalvin Manor or another — has shaped your identity, your values, or your goals. How do you plan to give back to the community that raised you?

Prompt 2 — Overcoming Adversity

This scholarship honors the resilience that defines the Montalvin Manor spirit. Describe a significant challenge you have faced — personal, academic, or financial — and how you have grown from that experience. What does perseverance mean to you?

Prompt 3 — Future Goals & Impact

Where do you see yourself in ten years, and how will your education get you there? Describe your academic or career goals and how earning this scholarship will help you achieve them. How do you hope to make a difference — in your field, your family, or your community?

Renewal

Scholarship Renewal Policy

Recipients may apply for renewal of the scholarship for up to two (2) additional academic years, provided they meet the following conditions:

  • Maintained a cumulative GPA of 3.8 or higher in the preceding academic year
  • Enrolled full-time (12+ credits) or part-time with documented extenuating circumstances
  • Submitted a brief renewal essay (250 words) describing academic progress and continued goals
  • Demonstrated continued financial need

Renewal applications follow the same annual deadline of April 15. Renewal is not guaranteed and is subject to available funds and committee review.
